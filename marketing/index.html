A clearer reason to reach out.

Instead of starting with a giant list and a generic pitch, BuildMeLeads begins with an observable profile weakness and carries that context into the first line.

Workflow

Each step preserves the business context, so the final message feels researched rather than mass-produced.

Find

Discover relevant local businesses

Search service categories and locations while retaining the public business details needed for qualification.

Qualify

Surface weak-profile signals

Prioritize businesses with signals such as a missing website, limited profile activity, or other detectable Google Business Profile gaps.

Write

Generate a short, human opener

Groq turns the business name, city, detected weakness, offer, and call to action into concise outreach—not a four-paragraph sales pitch.

Send

Choose the level of control

Use manual approval for reviewable drafts or explicitly enable an automatic campaign workflow, sending through a connected Gmail account or SendGrid.

Personalization grounded in the profile.

The model receives the lead’s scraped business context and the sender’s campaign settings. Generated copy is stored before it enters the sending workflow.

  • Specific inputsBusiness name, city, detected weakness, campaign offer, and call to action.
  • Brief by designA curiosity-driven opening plus qualifying context, with no inflated sales language.
  • Validated before sendEmpty fields, unresolved placeholders, duplicate contacts, and suppression matches are blocked automatically.

Automation with a brake pedal.

Both sending modes pass through the same safeguards. Customers remain responsible for the legality and relevance of their outreach.

Per-user send capsDaily and hourly limits protect each account independently.
Suppression hard blocksUnsubscribed recipients cannot be queued again.
Duplicate-contact lookbackRecent outreach is checked before every attempt.
Required unsubscribe linkEvery outgoing message must include a working opt-out path.
Randomized send delaysBackground delivery avoids inline request bursts.
Rate circuit breakersQueues can pause when bounce or complaint thresholds are crossed.
