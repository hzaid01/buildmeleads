Refund Policy

Effective and last updated: August 31, 2026. This Policy explains subscription cancellation and refund handling for BuildMeLeads.

BuildMeLeads is currently pre-launch. No checkout is active and no payment is collected today.
On this pagePre-launchCancellationRefund eligibilityPre-ordersHow to request help

1. Current pre-launch status

The public BuildMeLeads website does not currently sell subscriptions, accept deposits, or charge waitlist members. Joining the waitlist creates no purchase obligation.

2. Subscription cancellation after launch

When paid subscriptions become available, you may cancel at any time through the account cancellation route or by contacting us at support@example.com. Cancellation prevents the next renewal. Unless the checkout states otherwise, you will retain access until the end of the billing period already paid for.

Deleting an account, disconnecting an email provider, or stopping use does not automatically cancel a paid subscription unless the product or checkout expressly says it does.

3. Refund eligibility after launch

Subscriptions are generally non-refundable for billing periods that have begun, including partial months or unused time after cancellation. We do not provide prorated refunds merely because the Service was not used.

We will provide a refund or other remedy where required by applicable law, where the payment provider’s mandatory buyer protections apply, where there is a qualifying duplicate or unauthorized charge, or where a material defect cannot be resolved within a reasonable period. Nothing in this Policy limits non-waivable consumer rights.

4. Any future pre-order

BuildMeLeads does not accept pre-orders today. If we later offer a paid pre-order before delivering access, the checkout will identify it clearly, state the expected delivery terms, and allow cancellation and a full refund before delivery as required by the applicable payment-provider terms and law.

5. How to request cancellation or a refund review

Email support@example.com with the account email, transaction or invoice identifier, the reason for your request, and any relevant evidence. Do not send full card details. We aim to acknowledge requests within five business days; payment-provider processing time may apply after approval.

If a merchant of record processes the transaction, you may also use the support or receipt links provided. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible.
