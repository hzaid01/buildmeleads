# BuildMeLeads brand guidelines

## Brand idea

**Find the weak signal. Start the right conversation.** BuildMeLeads turns visible gaps in a local business profile into a clear, relevant reason for an agency to reach out.

The visual world is a **service-area dispatch desk**: route cards, field notes, signal bars, precise checklists, and deliberate handoffs. It should feel operational and trustworthy, not futuristic or hype-driven.

## Voice

- Clear, observant, and useful.
- Curious rather than accusatory: say “I noticed…” rather than “Your profile is bad.”
- Specific rather than inflated: name the profile signal and the next step.
- Human, brief, and calm. Avoid “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” “10x,” and generic AI language.
- Honest about product status. Label planned prices, illustrative examples, and pre-launch capabilities.

## Core messages

1. Find local service businesses with observable Google Business Profile weaknesses.
2. Carry that context into short, personalized outreach.
3. Send through Gmail or SendGrid using manual or explicitly enabled automatic workflows.
4. Apply suppression, unsubscribe, duplicate, cap, delay, bounce, and complaint safeguards before sending.

## Identity

The route-card mark combines a structured business record, a weak-signal indicator, and a forward dispatch arrow. Avoid generic location pins, sparkles, brain icons, and abstract startup blobs.

## Color

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mineral ink | `#17211D` | Primary text and operational dark surfaces |
| Paper fog | `#EEF1EB` | Main public-site background |
| Utility green | `#28604D` | Trust, qualified states, and primary brand field |
| Signal yellow | `#F4C95D` | The only active accent; CTAs and weak-signal highlights |

Use signal yellow sparingly. Do not introduce purple, gradient text, or decorative gradients.

## Typography

- Display and body: Archivo, with clear weight changes and compact display tracking.
- Data and operational labels only: IBM Plex Mono.
- Keep body measures near 65–75 characters and avoid long centered text blocks.

## Interface behavior

- Accessible contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic heading order, and touch targets of at least 44px.
- One restrained reveal motion, with an equivalent static state for reduced-motion users.
- Cards exist only when the content is truly a discrete object, such as a lead route card.
- Never use fake testimonials, customer logos, dashboards, metrics, or availability claims.
